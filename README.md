## 🌌 About Me & Engineering Vision

I am a passionate **Software Engineer and Applied AI Systems Architect** with a strong dedication to engineering scalable, high-concurrency backend architectures, autonomous multi-agent systems, and educational platforms with measurable real-world impact.

- 🎓 **Academic Trajectory:** Independent Researcher & Student at Tashkent State Agrarian University (TDAU), actively preparing for graduate studies at **MIT EECS (Electrical Engineering & Computer Science)**.
- 💡 **Core Philosophy:** *Code is not just syntax; it is high-leverage infrastructure that solves tangible societal problems at scale.*
- 🔬 **Research & Technical Interests:**
  - High-Throughput Asynchronous Concurrency (Python `asyncio`, FastHTTP, Redis event queues)
  - Multi-Agent AI Architectures & Intelligent Autonomous Workflows
  - Database Scalability & Connection Pooling (PostgreSQL, Neon Cloud Serverless)
  - Full-Stack Edge Delivery & Zero-Latency Frontends (React 19, Vite, Vercel CDN)
- 🚀 **Current Mission:** Engineering production-grade open-source ecosystems that bridge the gap between intelligent AI systems and large-scale educational communities.
